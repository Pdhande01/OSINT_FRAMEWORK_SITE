About the Creator

Driven by Curiosity & Ethical Reconnaissance

Driven by a deep passion for cybersecurity, ethical hacking, and open-source intelligence (OSINT), I created the OSINT Field Guide Framework to solve a persistent challenge in investigative reconnaissance: the fragmentation of investigative utilities across scattered scripts, repositories, and individual platforms.

Whether analyzing domain infrastructure, tracking digital footprints, verifying threat intelligence indicators, or performing geospatial analysis, having organized, actionable tooling is vital. This project was developed to systematize raw field research into an accessible, high-performance reference architecture for ethical security researchers, digital investigators, and cyber professionals.
